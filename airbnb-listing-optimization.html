Frequently asked

Everything hosts ask before they hire us

How does Airbnb's algorithm work?
Airbnb's search algorithm evaluates roughly 800 signals to decide which listings appear first for a given search. The biggest categories are booking probability (how likely a guest is to click and convert), listing quality (photos, completeness, response rate, reviews, cancellation rate), and personalization to the searcher's preferences. Since the 2025 algorithm overhaul, conversion velocity replaced the old new-listing boost, meaning ranking is now earned, not granted.
Why is my Airbnb not getting bookings?
If you're getting impressions but no bookings, the problem is conversion, not visibility. The most common causes are a weak cover photo (low click-through), a title that doesn't match the keywords guests search, missing amenity tags that disqualify you from filtered searches, and pricing that's misaligned with your competitive set. We diagnose all four in the first hour of an audit.
How long does listing optimization take to show results?
Most hosts see measurable changes in search ranking and click-through rate within 14–21 days of the optimization going live. Booking lift typically follows in the first 30 days. We send a baseline performance snapshot the day we launch and a results report at the 30-day mark so you can verify the lift directly.
What does your listing optimization service include?
Title rewrite, cover-photo selection and A/B testing, photo order and editing, full description rewrite, complete amenities mapping (against all 130+ Airbnb tags), pricing strategy with length-of-stay discount logic and weekend rules, promotion configuration, daily search-rank tracking from multiple traveler-origin markets, and a monthly performance report. Everything is done for you, you approve, we ship.
How much does Airbnb listing optimization cost?
We price per listing: a flat monthly rate plus a one-time listing optimization fee per property — never a percentage of your revenue. The fee covers the full audit, market analysis, and account setup, and larger portfolios get discounted per-listing rates. Tell us what you run and we'll quote your exact rate on one short call, before you commit to anything.
Can I optimize my Airbnb listing myself?
Yes, and many hosts do successfully. The pieces that benefit most from a professional are (1) cover photo selection (it's the single biggest conversion lever and easy to get wrong), (2) the keyword research behind your title and description, and (3) competitive pricing strategy. If you want to DIY, our blog and free Airbnb ranking tool will get you most of the way.
Do you only work with Airbnb, or also VRBO and Booking.com?
Airbnb is our primary platform, that's where the conversion gains compound fastest because of the algorithm's behavior. We do mirror optimizations to VRBO and Booking.com listings on request, though the algorithm dynamics are different and the lift is generally smaller.
Do I need to be a Superhost already?
No. About a third of our clients aren't Superhosts when they start. Superhost status is itself a ranking signal we work toward, it's tied to response rate, cancellation rate, and rating thresholds, all of which are downstream of the optimizations we make.
What if my listing is brand new with no reviews?
New listings have a different optimization playbook because the 2025 algorithm change removed the old new-listing boost. The priority shifts to driving fast initial bookings via aggressive launch pricing, an exceptional cover photo, and complete amenity coverage so the listing isn't filtered out. We've launched dozens of new listings, happy to walk through the playbook on the strategy call.
How is this different from tools like Rankbreeze or AutoRank?
Tools like Rankbreeze and AutoRank are software, they show you the data and give you suggestions, but you still execute. RevWhisper is a managed service: we use those signals plus our own daily ranking checks to actually rewrite, deploy, and iterate on your listing for you. The combination is what most hosts actually need, data without execution rarely moves the needle.

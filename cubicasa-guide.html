Floor Plans with CubiCasa
RevWhisper

A floor plan is one of the cheapest conversion lifts on an STR listing. Guests who can't picture the layout hesitate, and hesitation costs bookings. CubiCasa turns a 5-minute phone scan into a professionally rendered 3D floor plan. We upload it as a photo in your Airbnb gallery, where guests already scrolling can see exactly how the spaces connect.

Which CubiCasa tier? CubiCasa offers Standard ($10), Plus ($20), and Plus 3D ($65). For Airbnb listings, use Plus 3D. It is the only tier that produces a furnished, full-color 3D floor plan, which is what performs best in the photo gallery and gives guests a clear picture of the layout.

How It Works

1

Download & Sign Up

Get CubiCasa on iOS or Android. Create an account and select the Plus 3D plan ($65 per scan). Takes about one minute.

2

Walk & Scan

Phone at chest height, walk slowly through every room. About 5 minutes for a typical home. The app uploads automatically when you finish.

3

CubiCasa Processes

You'll get a finished, furnished 3D floor plan emailed to you within 48 hours. Download it from your CubiCasa account.

4

Send It to Us

Forward the finished plan to RevWhisper. We upload it to your Airbnb gallery and any other platforms where it helps conversions.

Why It Matters for Your Listing

Reduces booking hesitation

Groups and families want to know "who sleeps where" before they commit. A floor plan answers that instantly and privately, without a single message to the host.

Highlights square footage

A "2,400 sq ft" number means little in the abstract. A visual layout makes scale obvious and helps justify a higher nightly rate.

Lives in the photo gallery

Airbnb treats the floor plan like any other photo. Dropped into position 6 or 7 of the gallery, it catches every guest already scrolling without needing a separate field to hunt for.

Accessibility signal

Guests filtering for step-free layouts, wide hallways, or ground-floor bedrooms can self-qualify from the plan. Fewer refund requests down the line.

Scan Tips for the Cleanest Result

  • Scan the whole property in one continuous session, including all floors
  • Start at the lowest-floor entrance and work upward
  • Hold the phone in landscape at chest height, both hands, camera forward
  • Stay 5 to 11 ft (1.5 to 3.5 m) from walls and furniture
  • Walk at a normal pace. No sudden turns or jumps between floors
  • Open interior doors, close exterior doors, raise all blinds
  • Skip closets under 3x3 ft. Stand in the doorway and pan
  • If tracking drops, return to the last mapped corner to recover

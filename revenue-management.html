01

Set-and-forget rates that don't move with demand.

Most hosts pick a nightly rate once and adjust it twice a year. Demand moves daily — by event, by weather, by competitor calendars filling up. A flat rate is the single most expensive habit in short-term rental.

02

Min-stays you set once and never revisited.

The right minimum-stay isn't a number — it's a schedule. Two nights for shoulder weekends. One for orphan gaps. Three for holidays. Most listings carry the same 2-night rule all year and quietly leak occupancy on both ends.

03

Smart Pricing underprices your peaks.

Airbnb's built-in pricing optimizes for occupancy, not revenue. It drops your rate to fill the calendar — which is exactly the wrong move on the dates you should be charging the most.

Minimum Stay Strategy

Comp Set Monitoring

Seasonal Calendar

Last-minute discounts that catch the bookings you'd otherwise miss. Weekly and monthly tiers tuned to your turnover cost. Opportunistic promotions when a comp set softens. We deploy and pull these every week — not once at setup.

FAQ

Common Questions

What does an Airbnb revenue manager do?
An Airbnb revenue manager sets and adjusts your nightly rate, minimum stays, and length-of-stay discounts based on demand, seasonality, local events, and competitor pricing. The goal is to maximize total revenue across the calendar — not just the headline nightly rate. RevWhisper does this for you nightly, using PriceLabs as the pricing engine plus human judgment.
How much does Airbnb revenue management cost?
Most independent Airbnb revenue managers charge 5–8% of your gross revenue for small portfolios, or 1–3% for larger ones. RevWhisper charges a flat monthly rate per listing — never a percentage of your revenue — with discounts for larger portfolios. Your quote includes revenue management, listing copy and SEO, photo direction, and daily search-rank tracking — not just pricing.
Do I need a revenue manager if I already use Airbnb Smart Pricing?
Airbnb's Smart Pricing tends to underprice peak dates and overweight occupancy. It does not consider your competitive set, local events, length-of-stay strategy, or minimum-stay calibration. Most hosts who switch from Smart Pricing to a real revenue management strategy see a 10–40% revenue lift on the same listing.
What pricing tools do you use?
We use PriceLabs as our pricing engine. The tool is a small part of the work. Configuring base rates, min/max guardrails, customizations, length-of-stay discounts, minimum-stay rules, and orphan-night logic — and revisiting all of it monthly — is where revenue management actually happens. We do that for you.
How much can dynamic pricing increase Airbnb revenue?
Hosts who switch from a flat nightly rate to a properly tuned dynamic pricing strategy typically see a 10–40% revenue lift, with the average around 22%. Hosts who adjust prices at least once a month earn roughly 13% more than hosts who set rates and forget.
When will I see results from RevWhisper revenue management?
Pricing and minimum-stay changes go live within the first week of onboarding. Most hosts see measurable booking-pace and revenue improvements inside the first 30 days. Compounding gains — better seasonal positioning, refined length-of-stay strategy, smarter promotions — show up over months two and three.

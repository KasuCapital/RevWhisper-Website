The Reality

Airbnb supply is increasing.
Your rank isn't.

The old game is over

Two years ago, your listing competed against other individual hosts. People adjusting prices by feel, refreshing photos when they remembered, writing descriptions once and hoping for the best. That market is gone.

The new competition

Institutional operators now manage over 30% of top-performing short-term rentals in most major markets. They deploy revenue management teams, professional photographers on retainer, algorithmic pricing that updates 340+ times per day, and A/B-tested listing copy refined across thousands of properties.

The algorithm shifted

Airbnb restructured its algorithm. Static listings that don't demonstrate consistent conversion momentum, fresh content signals, and competitive pricing responsiveness are being deprioritized. A fraction of a position per week. Invisible until you check your bookings three months later and realize what happened.

Nothing changed. Everything around you did.

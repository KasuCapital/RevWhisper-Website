02 / The Revamp

What used to be. What it is now.

Here is Woods Edge the week before we started and after its photo update, with its title and description before and after the rewrite.

The first five photos. The cover moves from a dusk view of the house and fire pit to a golden-hour shot of the house and its deck. The first five now show the theater room, the deck, the hot tub and the game room.

Title · before
Woods Edge Private 5 Acres Hot Tub Fire Pit
Title · after
Hot Tub, Fire Pit, Theater, Games & EV | Sleeps 8
Description · before
Escape to Woods Edge, a serene and inviting retreat for up to 8 guests, nestled deep in the woods with no neighbors in sight and just 5 minutes from town.
Description · after
“Exactly like the pictures this home was the perfect spot for our large group of friends to land.” That’s how a guest described Woods Edge on 5.5 wooded acres in Barryville.
Why we changed it

The old title led with acreage. The new one leads with what groups do there: the hot tub, the fire pit, the theater room and the games, plus the EV charger. When we started, groups of six to eight made up about 60% of its bookings, so “Sleeps 8” went into the title and the description opens with a review from a large group of friends.

The new title and description are both live on Airbnb.

03 / What We Changed

What we changed, in plain terms.

The problem. Well-kept cabins with strong reviews, but hard to find. One showed up in fewer than one search in twenty.
  • Set a lowest nightly price and a minimum stay for each of the 17 homes. Nine of them came with a revenue guarantee, tracked from day one.
  • Rewrote Woods Edge’s title and description, and gave it a new golden-hour cover photo.
  • Priced fall foliage weekends early.
  • Filled the weeknights around them at short notice, and booked holiday weeks in October at holiday rates.

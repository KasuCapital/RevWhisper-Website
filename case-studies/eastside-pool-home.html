02 / The Revamp

What used to be. What it is now.

The listing before and after its photo update, and its title and description before and after the rewrite.

The first five photos. The cover is now a brighter daylight view of the curved white front. The pool moves into the second slot, and the living room joins the first five.

Title · before
Eastside Lux Group Retreat W/ Pool near Downtown
Title · after
Eastside Pool Home • Outdoor Fireplace • Game Room
Description · before
Welcome to your private urban oasis in the heart of East Austin where modern comfort meets laid-back Texas charm.
Description · after
“We had 12 guests and the house never felt cramped.” That’s how a guest described Holly St, our three-story Eastside home in the Holly neighborhood.
Why we changed it

The old title called it a “Lux Group Retreat,” which tells a guest very little. The new one names three things guests can picture: the pool, the outdoor fireplace and the game room. Groups of 16 and 12 are among its five most common bookings, so the description opens with a guest whose group of 12 said the house never felt cramped.

The new title and description are both live on Airbnb.

Pool

Pool. The same shot of the pool, brightened and warmed up.

03 / What We Changed

What we changed, in plain terms.

The problem. A new house with spotless reviews, sitting on page 4 of Airbnb search for some of its late-summer dates, where few guests ever look.
  • Never priced a night below the owners’ $250 minimum.
  • Retitled the listing around the pool, outdoor fireplace and game room, and rewrote the description to open with a guest’s review of a 12-guest stay.
  • Swapped in a daylight cover and moved the pool into the second photo.
  • Caught a weekend priced nearly $300 a night above similar homes, lowered open weekday nights just enough to reach page one of Airbnb search, and kept weekends higher.

03 / What We Changed

What we changed, in plain terms.

The problem. Well-reviewed homes from a ten-year Superhost, in a city adding competition fast: four-bedroom homes on sale for November stays rose from 45 to 77. In its first tracked week, Coconut Grove Villa sat around #246 in our searches.
  • Checked the extra charges added to the price on each booking site, and never priced below any owner’s minimum.
  • Retitled all six listings and rewrote all six descriptions to open with guest reviews.
  • Replaced cover photos so each listing leads with its strongest shot.
  • Set prices date by date for open weekdays, allowed shorter stays on gaps too short for the usual minimum, and kept event and holiday weeks at full price.

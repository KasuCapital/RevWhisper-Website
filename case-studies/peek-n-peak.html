02 / The Revamp

What used to be. What it is now.

The listing before and after its photo update, and its title and description before and after the rewrite.

The first five photos. The chalet stays on the cover, now in a brighter, higher-angle shot. The aerial over the golf course moves up to the second slot, and the covered balcony replaces the porch shot.

Nestled at the top of the hill inside the beautiful Peek n’ Peak Resort; We are sure you will enjoy our beautiful 4 Bedroom Condo thats directly on the golf course and a few feet from the ski lift.
Description · after
“Our stay was magical and made Christmas feel like a Hallmark movie.” That’s how a guest described Peek n’ Peak, our 4-bedroom condo at the top of the hill inside the Peek n’ Peak Resort.
Why we changed it

The old title said nothing about where the condo sits. The new one names the two reasons people come, ski and golf, plus the game room. Its bookings are mostly big groups: 14 guests is its most common party size, and 45% of stays include children. Guests book about three months ahead on average, so the description opens with a family’s Christmas review and answers what large groups ask before booking: resort fees, parking and the drive in.

The new description is live on Airbnb.

03 / What We Changed

What we changed, in plain terms.

The problem. Already near the top of search in May, but empty all of last October and November: 61 nights, none booked.
  • Never priced a night below the owners’ minimum.
  • Rewrote the description to open with a guest’s Christmas review, with a room-by-room breakdown and answers on resort fees, parking and the drive in.
  • Moved the golf course aerial into the first five photos.
  • Left weekend prices alone where they were already booking, lowered prices toward that minimum only on open midweek and last-minute nights, and priced ski weekends months ahead.

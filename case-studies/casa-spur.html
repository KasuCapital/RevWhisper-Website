Not every month is ahead yet. October booked 48% of its nights against 52% for similar homes nearby. November is at $8.7K so far, against $10.5K last November, and January is still filling. December is the standout: 74% booked against 37% for similar homes.

02 / What We Changed

In the first weeks, in plain terms.

  • Replaced the photo set before launch, with a brighter aerial cover that shows the pool, court and putting green together.
  • Led each room with its strongest shot, like the backyard fire pit lounge.
  • On our first day, reset the base nightly price from $1,225 to $1,125, and kept every night above the owner’s $700 minimum.
  • Lowered mid-October nights by about $140 where the home sat deep in Airbnb search, around #73 to #197. Those nights then booked.
  • Used short Airbnb promotions on open nights around Halloween weekend, and allowed shorter minimum stays to fill gaps between bookings.

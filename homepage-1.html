The problem

A well-priced listing on page three still loses.

01

When did you last check your ranking?

Most hosts obsess over nightly rates. Airbnb's algorithm weighs conversion velocity, response time, and booking momentum — position decides who gets seen.

02

The algorithm changed. Did your listing?

Airbnb overhauled its ranking system in 2025. The new-listing boost is gone; conversion-based weighting replaced it. Hosts who didn't adapt are being quietly buried.

03

Your competition isn't other hosts anymore.

Revenue management companies run dynamic pricing, automated messaging, and A/B-tested copy across thousands of properties. The bar moved. Your listing has to move with it.

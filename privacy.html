1. Who we are

RevWhisper is a brand of MCO CORP. We help short-term rental hosts increase listing performance through pricing, content, and photo optimization. You can reach us any time at Admin@RevWhisper.com. We are the controller responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request a free audit, book a strategy call, or enroll, you may provide:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number;
  • Your Airbnb (or other short-term rental) listing links;
  • Details about your situation — for example, how many listings you host and the issue you want help with;
  • Your acceptance of our Service Agreement at enrollment; and
  • Anything else you choose to send us, such as the contents of an email.

Information we collect automatically

When you visit the site, we and our analytics providers may collect:

  • Device and browser type, approximate location derived from your IP address, and similar technical data;
  • Pages you view, links you click, and how you move through the site;
  • The website that referred you; and
  • Marketing campaign parameters (such as UTM tags) so we know which ad or link brought you here.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you enroll, your card details are entered on Stripe’s secure checkout — we never receive or store your full card number. We retain a record of your transaction (such as plan, amount, and confirmation status) for accounting and support.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Prepare and deliver your listing audit and walkthrough call;
  • Contact you about your audit, your call, your enrollment, or your account;
  • Provide, operate, and improve our services;
  • Process payments and manage your subscription;
  • Measure and improve our marketing, including which campaigns work;
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats; and
  • Comply with our legal obligations.

4. Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar browser storage to run the site, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. When you first visit, a banner lets you Accept or Decline analytics and advertising cookies. Essential storage needed to operate the site — such as remembering your cookie choice and the campaign that referred you — is always active.

The main technologies we use are:

  • Google Analytics 4 — to measure site traffic and usage. Analytics only loads after you accept the cookie banner.
  • Meta Pixel — to measure ad performance and reach relevant audiences on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Vercel — privacy-friendly performance and visit metrics for the hosting platform.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site’s storage in your browser, which brings the banner back, or by using the browser and platform controls described in Advertising & analytics choices.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as described here:

  • Service providers who work on our behalf — including Stripe (payments), Google (analytics), Meta (advertising measurement), Make.com (routing your form submissions into our CRM), and Vercel (hosting). They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Legal and safety — when we believe disclosure is required by law, or is necessary to enforce our agreements or protect the rights, safety, and property of RevWhisper, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers — if RevWhisper is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not sell or rent the contact details you submit. Your name, email, phone number, and listing links are used to prepare your audit and reach you about your call — never sold or traded. We do use the advertising and analytics cookies described above; you can opt out of those at any time.

6. Advertising & analytics choices

You have several ways to control measurement and advertising:

  • Our cookie banner — choose Decline to keep analytics from loading.
  • Google — opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, and manage ad personalization in your Google Ads Settings.
  • Meta — manage ad preferences in your Facebook Ad Preferences or Instagram settings.
  • Your browser — block or delete cookies, and many browsers offer a “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signal, which we honor where required by law.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services and for the purposes described in this policy — for example, while you are a client and for a reasonable period afterward — and as required to meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

8. How we protect your information

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, and we limit access to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, email us at Admin@RevWhisper.com and we will respond as required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

  • California residents — you may request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, request deletion, and opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. As noted above, we do not sell the contact details you submit.
  • EEA & UK residents — you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Third-party sites & platforms

Our site links to and works alongside third-party platforms such as Airbnb and other booking sites, our application at app.revwhisper.com, and our scheduling and payment providers. This policy does not cover those third parties. Please review their privacy policies, since their handling of your information is governed by their own terms.

11. Children’s privacy

RevWhisper is a service for businesses and property hosts. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International visitors

RevWhisper is operated from the United States. If you access the site from outside the U.S., your information will be processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will be made clear on this page. Your continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

14. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? We’re happy to help.

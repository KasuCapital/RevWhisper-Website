What happens on the call

Here's how the 30 minutes go.

  1. 1
    We get to know your property. Where it stands today, where you're trying to take it, and the number you believe it could hit. There's a lot we can't see from our side, so this is where you fill it in.
  2. 2
    We pull up your audit, live. We screen-share exactly where you're ranking right now across your real availability, and where your revenue opportunity is. This is the part most "free audits" skip.
  3. 3
    We show you what we'd do. A straight walk-through of RevWhisper and, if it's a fit, what working together looks like. If it's not, you still leave knowing exactly where you stand.
The part hosts remember

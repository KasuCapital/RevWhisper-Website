How Airbnb Displays Your Photos

Airbnb organizes photos into a Photo Tour grouped by room. Each room shows one large hero image with two smaller supporting shots below it.

The hero shot and first 5 images drive roughly 80% of all photo views. They determine whether a guest keeps scrolling or bounces. The first 5 is the single biggest lever on listing conversion.

Orientation and Layout

The mix of landscape and portrait photos determines which grid layout Airbnb builds for each room:

3 landscape

Landscape hero on top, two landscape supports side-by-side below.

1 portrait + 2 landscape

Portrait hero on one side, two landscape supports stacked beside it.

3 portrait

Portrait hero on one side, two smaller portraits stacked beside it.

2 portrait + 1 landscape avoid

Visually awkward. We don't set rooms up this way.

Takeaway: shoot the strongest angle in both landscape and portrait. Aim for 3 landscapes, 3 portraits, or 1 portrait + 2 landscapes per room.

Aim for 2 of each per room (6 total). The hero sets the scene, the secondary adds context, and the close-up proves the details.

Drone Coverage

For properties where scale or setting is part of the appeal, drone gives guests a sense of place that no ground-level shot can. This often becomes the hero shot and search thumbnail.

When it makes sense: waterfront, pool-centric, acreage with privacy/views, large outdoor amenity footprints. Skip it for urban condos, standard suburban lots, or properties where neighbors are the main thing visible from above.

Top-down: shows full amenity layout and how spaces connect.

Angled: captures scale, privacy, and amenity spread. Often becomes the hero shot.

Accessibility Photos

Airbnb has a separate accessibility section reviewed by their team. Verified photos unlock accessibility search filters. If the property has any of these features, capture them:

Doorways wider than 32 inches

Tape measure on inside of door frame, numbers visible, door fully open. Needed for guest entrance, each bedroom, each bathroom.

Step-free path to entrance

Photos every 10 feet from parking to front door. All doors/gates open. Ramps in place. Separate entrance shot from 8 feet away.

Lit path to entrance

Taken at dusk or evening showing all outdoor light sources along the path. Exclude interior lighting.

Step-free room access

Camera angled down to show threshold. Door open. Camera outside looking in. Thresholds under 2 inches.

Step-free shower

Full shower visible including where floor meets bathroom floor. Threshold under 1 inch.

Grab bars (shower and toilet)

Clear view showing bar is wall-mounted (not towel rack or suction). Show location relative to fixture.

Shower or bath chair

Full seat visible inside shower or tub. Fixed or freestanding.

Disabled parking spot

Signage/markings for public spots. Private: show spot is 11+ ft wide and distance to entrance.

Room-by-Room Guidance

6 photos minimum per room. Multiple angles, every amenity a guest would want to verify.

Exterior 6 min

Full property from 2+ angles. Front entry. Twilight with all lights on. Drone if available. Driveway/parking.

Living Room 6 min

Full room from 2 positions. Kitchen/dining connection. TV setup. Fireplace. Seating capacity.

Kitchen 6 min

Full layout from 2 angles. Coffee station. Appliances. Island/bar seating. Storage.

Dining Area 6 min

Table set for full guest count from 2 angles. Place setting close-up. Lighting/view.

Each Bedroom 6 min each

Full room from 2 positions. Bedding close-up. Nightstand/desk. View. Closet. Every bedroom needs coverage.

Each Bathroom 6 min each

Full bathroom from doorway. Shower/tub. Vanity and mirror. Towel setup. Special fixtures.

Outdoor Spaces 6+ min

Drone or elevated wide. Each feature: pool, hot tub, fire pit, grill, dining, lounge. Twilight with string lights.

Game Room / Bonus 6 min

Full room showing all equipment. Each game close-up. Equipment staged ready to play.

Playground / Courts 6 min

Wide shot of full setup. Equipment close-ups. Gear staged and ready.

Entry / Parking 3 min

Entry from inside. Parking showing capacity. Accessibility path if applicable.

Scheduling the Shoot

9 – 11 AM
Soft angled light. Best for interiors.
2 – 4 PM
Warm light. Also great for interiors.
Sunset + 15–40 min
Twilight exteriors. All lights on. Tripod.

Overcast days are ideal. Plan for 2–3 hours for a standard property, longer for 5+ bedrooms. Stay through twilight for exteriors.

Staging Checklist

Set Up Before Every Shot

  • All blinds, curtains, and shades fully open
  • Every lamp on for warm ambient light
  • Beds made with white/light linens, comforter folded back, 4-5 pillows
  • Couch pillows fluffed, one throw draped on arm
  • Dining table set for full guest count
  • Kitchen counters clear except coffee setup, cutting board, fruit bowl
  • Bathrooms: fresh white towels, hand soap, small plant or candle
  • Every toilet lid closed
  • Outdoor furniture with clean cushions, string lights on
  • Game equipment organized, set up ready to play

Remove Before Every Shot

  • Family photos, personal items, religious or political items
  • Charging cables, visible cords, power strips
  • Trash cans, shoes, scattered remotes
  • Dish rack, sponges, dish soap, paper towels, fridge magnets
  • All personal products, medications, shampoo bottles
  • Toilet brush, plunger, bathroom trash bin
  • Cars from driveway, hoses, toys, tools, trash bins
  • Lockbox from the door
  • Check every mirror and reflective surface for reflections

How to Price Your Airbnb: A Pricing Guide for Hosts

Most hosts set a nightly rate once and adjust it when bookings slow down. Here is how pricing on Airbnb actually works, and the six settings that decide what you earn.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 16, 2026

Price your Airbnb night by night instead of using one flat rate. Set a weekday base rate from 5 to 7 comparable listings, add separate weekend and holiday rates, then manage the calendar with minimum stays, gap night rules and length-of-stay discounts. Recheck those settings whenever demand changes, or have a tool like PriceLabs or a revenue manager do it daily.

How does Airbnb pricing work for hosts?

Guests search with dates and a guest count. Airbnb orders the results using price, ratings and reviews, and how often guests book each listing. Listings priced below similar homes nearby tend to rank higher, and a minimum stay longer than the guest's trip keeps you out of that search.

How do I set a base nightly rate for my Airbnb?

Search your own market in a logged-out browser and pick the 5 to 7 listings that match your bedroom count, property type and location. Price your ordinary weeknight within range of them. Our How to Run a Comp Set Analysis in 30 Minutes walks through it step by step.

Pro tip: Check comps 30, 60 and 90 days out. Prices close to the date show what is left unbooked, not what the market pays.

Should weekends and holidays have different Airbnb prices?

Yes. Set Friday and Saturday as separate rates, and set holidays, school breaks and local events separately again, because demand is not the same on every weekend. A single flat increase overprices some weekends and underprices others. Airbnb's weekend pricing lets you add a premium to your base price for Friday and Saturday nights, and you can select any night in your calendar to edit its price. Weekend pricing needs Smart Pricing turned off.

What minimum stay should I set on Airbnb?

Use different minimums by period. A two or three night minimum on weekends cuts turnovers and keeps one-night bookings from splitting your best dates. A shorter minimum on weekdays keeps you in more searches. Lower it further for single open nights between bookings. Airbnb lets you set a minimum stay based on the day of check-in, and a custom trip length for specific dates.

Common mistake: One minimum stay for the whole year. It keeps you out of short-trip searches in slow months and lets one-night bookings break up peak weekends.

How do I price gap nights between Airbnb bookings?

A single open night between two bookings rarely books at full price. Allow a one-night stay on that date and lower its rate. Airbnb's custom trip length lets you set a shorter minimum for certain dates, which it says can help fill gaps in your calendar.

Does Airbnb's 15.5% host-only fee change my prices?

Yes. Most hosts now pay a single 15.5% host-only fee, and Airbnb deducts the entire fee from your payout instead of splitting it with the guest, so the same nightly rate pays you less. The fee also applies to cleaning and pet fees. See The 15.5% Fee Squeeze for how to recalculate.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I change my Airbnb prices?

Review prices at least weekly for the next 90 days, and daily if you can. Dynamic pricing tools and revenue managers adjust rates every day based on demand.

Is it bad to lower my Airbnb price?

Not when it is done on purpose. A lower rate on a slow date can land a booking and a fresh review, which helps your ranking on dates that matter more.

What is a good base rate for my Airbnb?

A rate within range of 5 to 7 listings that match your bedroom count, property type and location. Your own costs matter for profit, but guests compare you to those listings, not to your mortgage.

Should I offer weekly and monthly discounts on Airbnb?

Usually yes, especially in slow months. Airbnb's weekly discount applies to stays of 7 nights or more and its monthly discount to stays of 28 nights or longer. Longer stays mean fewer turnovers and can fill weeks that would otherwise sit empty.

Airbnb Cancellation Policies for Hosts: Flexible, Moderate, Limited and Firm

Airbnb retired Strict and added Limited. What each policy pays you when a guest cancels, which one fits your calendar, and how to change it.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated October 28, 2026

Airbnb hosts now choose between four standard cancellation policies for stays under 28 nights: Flexible, Moderate, Limited and Firm, after Airbnb retired Strict for new selection on October 1, 2025. Firm is the strictest you can pick, with a full refund up to 30 days before check-in, and Limited sits in the middle at 14 days. Choose by how fast your cancelled dates refill: dates that rebook quickly suit Flexible or Moderate, and dates that stay empty suit Limited or Firm.

What are Airbnb's cancellation policies for hosts?

PolicyFull refund untilIf the guest cancels later
Flexible24 hours before check-inYou're paid for nights stayed plus one more night

Firm30 days before check-in50% of all nights from 7 to 30 days out; 100% inside 7 days

Every policy also has a 24-hour free cancellation window for bookings made at least 7 days before check-in. Stays of 28 nights or more use your separate long-term policy. Sources: Airbnb's Help Center and host resources, and channel manager summaries of the October 2025 changes.

What happened to Airbnb's Strict cancellation policy?

Airbnb stopped offering Strict for bookings made on or after October 1, 2025, and moved listings on Strict to Firm unless the host opted out before that date. It's now invitation-only. Super Strict 30 and Super Strict 60 were retired too: they stopped being available for new listings on June 15, 2026, and most listings on them moved to Firm on September 15, 2026.

Common mistake: Assuming a listing still runs on Strict. Check the policy shown in your listing settings, since most were moved to Firm.

What is the Limited cancellation policy on Airbnb?

Limited is the new middle option, added for bookings from October 1, 2025. Guests get a full refund until 14 days before check-in, 50% back between 7 and 14 days, and nothing inside 7 days. It gives hosts more protection than Moderate without the 30-day window of Firm.

Which Airbnb cancellation policy is best for hosts?

The one that matches how fast your dates refill. In busy markets where a cancelled weekend rebooks in days, Flexible or Moderate win more bookings and cost you little. Airbnb has said 40% of guests prioritize free cancellation when they book. Where cancelled dates stay empty, such as large homes, peak holiday weeks and seasonal markets, Limited or Firm protect revenue.

Pro tip: Reserve Now, Pay Later only appears on Flexible and Moderate listings, according to Hostfully's 2026 guide. If you use Limited or Firm, guests can't choose it on your listing.

Should my cancellation policy change by season?

It can. A ski home might run Firm through holiday weeks and Moderate in mud season, and a beach house might do the opposite. Check how far ahead each season books: the longer the booking window, the more a strict policy protects. RevWhisper sets the policy together with pricing and minimum stays for each season.

How do I change my cancellation policy on Airbnb?

Open the listing in your Airbnb host account, go to its policies or booking settings, and choose a new cancellation policy. The change applies to new bookings only; existing reservations keep the policy they were booked under.

What happens when the host cancels?

Host cancellations carry their own fees and can block dates on your calendar, and they count against Superhost status, which requires a cancellation rate under 1%. Set a policy you can live with rather than cancelling guests later.

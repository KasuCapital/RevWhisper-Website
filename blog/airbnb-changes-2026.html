Airbnb in 2026: Every Change Hosts Need to Know

A new fee model, new cancellation policies, built-in dynamic pricing and new house rules. Here is what changed on Airbnb in 2026, and what to do about each.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 12, 2026

2026 was the first full year on Airbnb's 15.5% host-only fee and its new cancellation lineup, both introduced in late 2025. During 2026, Airbnb retired Super Strict cancellation policies, launched built-in dynamic pricing in its September 30 Fall Update, and released new house rules that take effect November 20, 2026. Each change affects payouts, pricing or what you can ask of guests.

When did Airbnb switch to the 15.5% host-only fee?

Hosts connected to a property management system moved to the single 15.5% host-only fee on October 27, 2025, and most other hosts moved on December 1, 2025. In 2026, UK hosts moved to 15.5% on June 22. Hosts in Mexico pay 16%. The same nightly rate now pays you less unless you adjusted it. See The 15.5% Fee Squeeze.

Did Airbnb change cancellation policies?

Yes. On October 1, 2025, Airbnb retired the Strict policy for new selection, moved existing Strict listings to Firm unless hosts opted out, and added a new Limited policy. The standard choices are now Flexible, Moderate, Limited and Firm, with Firm the strictest. In 2026, Super Strict 30 and Super Strict 60 stopped being available for new listings on June 15, and most existing listings on them moved to Firm on September 15.

What is the 1099-K threshold for Airbnb hosts in 2026?

$20,000 and more than 200 transactions. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed July 4, 2025, restored that threshold and cancelled the planned $600 rule. Ask your accountant what it means for your filing.

How are guests booking Airbnbs now?

In Q4 2025, over 70% of eligible bookings used Reserve Now, Pay Later, and Airbnb expanded it to guests worldwide in early 2026. App bookings were 64% of all nights booked in Q4 2025, up from 60% a year earlier. See Reserve Now, Pay Later and Your Revenue Strategy and 64% of Airbnb Nights Are Now Booked on the App.

What did Airbnb's 2026 Fall Update add for hosts?

Announced September 30, 2026: built-in dynamic pricing (rolling out from October), an AI earnings dashboard for hosts with five or fewer listings, and a multi-listing calendar for hosts with six or more. Separately, since June 2026, hosts can offer an optional 15% discount to top-rated guests, meaning guests rated 4.8 or higher with at least three reviews.

What are Airbnb's new house rules for 2026?

Airbnb released new policies on October 21, 2026, effective November 20, 2026. Hosts can ask guests to put dishes in the sink or dishwasher, gather used towels, and separate trash and recycling. Hosts can't require guests to wash and put away dishes, strip beds and start laundry, or take trash off site.

Pro tip: Update your checkout message before November 20. A request that breaks the new rules can cost you a review.

How did the short-term rental market do in 2026?

AirDNA's 2026 midyear outlook forecast US occupancy of 57.4%, above the pre-pandemic average, with revenue per available rental up 2.9%, driven mostly by higher nightly rates. Higher mortgage rates slowed new supply.

Frequently asked questions

What is Airbnb's host fee in 2026?

15.5% for most hosts on the host-only fee, which most hosts moved to by December 1, 2025. Some countries differ, such as Mexico at 16%.

When do Airbnb's new house rules start?

November 20, 2026. Review your house rules and checkout instructions before then.

Can Airbnb hosts still ask guests to clean?

Hosts can ask for light tasks such as putting dishes in the sink or dishwasher and gathering used towels. They can't require washing dishes, stripping beds and starting laundry, or taking trash off site.

Does Airbnb have its own dynamic pricing now?

Yes. It was announced September 30, 2026, with rollout starting in October.

What should hosts do before 2027?

Recalculate rates for the host fee, update house rules, set 2027 holiday rates, and decide which pricing tool to use.

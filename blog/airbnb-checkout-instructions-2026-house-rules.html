Airbnb Checkout Instructions That Follow the New 2026 House Rules

Airbnb's new house rules start November 20, 2026. What you can still ask guests to do at checkout, and a ready-to-copy checkout message that stays inside the rules.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated October 27, 2026

From November 20, 2026, Airbnb hosts can ask guests to put dishes in the sink or dishwasher, gather used towels, separate trash and recycling and turn off appliances, but can't require washing dishes, stripping beds, starting laundry or taking trash off site. Checkout instructions should list only the allowed tasks, plus practical steps such as locking up. Below is a message you can copy.

What can Airbnb hosts ask guests to do at checkout?

AllowedNot allowed
Put dishes in the sink or dishwasherWash dishes and put them away
Gather used towelsStrip beds or start laundry
Separate trash and recyclingTake trash off site
Turn off appliances like ACStop using listed amenities

Source: Airbnb's policy update announced October 21, 2026, effective November 20, 2026, as reported in coverage of the change.

What should my Airbnb checkout message say?

Checkout is at 11 a.m. Before you go, please:

  • Put dishes in the sink or dishwasher
  • Leave used towels in the bathroom
  • Put trash and recycling in the bins by the door
  • Turn off the AC or heat and the lights
  • Lock the front door and return the key to the lockbox

Thanks for staying with us. Safe travels.

Can I still charge for a messy checkout?

Damage and policy violations are handled through Airbnb's usual processes. Tasks beyond the allowed list can't be required, so build normal cleaning into your cleaning fee and turnover plan.

Do the new rules change my cleaning fee?

Possibly. If guests used to strip beds or start laundry, your cleaner now does it. Recheck your turnover time and cost, and your nightly pricing with it, since Airbnb's 15.5% host-only fee applies to your cleaning fee too.

Frequently asked questions

When do Airbnb's new house rules start?

November 20, 2026.

Can I ask Airbnb guests to wash dishes?

No. You can ask them to put dishes in the sink or dishwasher.

Can I ask guests to strip the beds?

No. You can ask them to gather used towels.

Can I ask guests to take out the trash?

You can ask them to separate trash and recycling, but not to take it off site.

Where do I edit checkout instructions?

In your listing's arrival and checkout settings on Airbnb.

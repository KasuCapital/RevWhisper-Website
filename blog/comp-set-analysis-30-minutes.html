How to Run a Comp Set Analysis in 30 Minutes

Your listing is invisible because you don't know who you're up against. A comp set analysis finds the listings taking your bookings in 30 minutes.

By RevWhisper TeamMarch 25, 2026Last updated October 27, 2026

A comp set analysis compares your Airbnb with the 5 to 7 listings guests see next to yours for the same dates, guest count and size. Search logged out, pick listings that match your bedroom count and property type, then compare cover photos, titles, amenities, prices and recent reviews. It takes about 30 minutes and shows what to fix first.

What do I need before I start a comp set analysis?

Your listing is invisible because you do not know who you are competing against. A comp set analysis shows you the listings stealing your bookings and exactly what they are doing differently. It takes 30 minutes and tells you why your views are not converting.

Before you start, have two things ready:

  • Your listing URL and your current ranking position for your primary search
  • An incognito browser window, so Airbnb shows you unbiased rankings

How do I search like a guest to see my real competition?

Open an incognito window. Search your city, your dates and your guest count. Scroll through the first 20 listings and screenshot the grid. These are your competitors. Your listing should be in this set. If it is not, your ranking problem is severe. This step takes about 5 minutes.

Pro tip: Search on the mobile app and on desktop separately. Results can differ between the app and the website, and 64% of Airbnb nights were booked on the app in Q4 2025.

Common mistake: Searching while logged into your host account, which skews the results and hides your true ranking position.

How do I pick my true competitors?

From those 20 results, pick the 5 to 7 listings that match your property type and bedroom count and are priced within 20% of your rate. Ignore everything else. These are your comp set. Write down their listing IDs or save the URLs. This takes about 5 minutes.

Pro tip: If most of your comps are Guest Favorites and you are not, that badge gap is likely costing you bookings in that search position.

Common mistake: Comparing yourself to listings with a different bedroom count or property type. It gives you useless data and the wrong conclusions.

What should I compare in their cover photos and titles?

Open each comp listing and look at its cover photo and title. Count how many have exterior shots, lifestyle staging, bright natural light, and titles with a unique selling point or a location callout. Then compare them to yours. The click happens or dies here, and the 12-item photography audit goes deeper on this step. Allow about 7 minutes.

Pro tip: Exterior cover photos showing views or curb appeal often stand out in the grid.

Common mistake: Using an interior-only cover photo when your exterior or view is your strongest asset and would set you apart in the grid.

Which amenities are my competitors listing that I'm not?

Scroll to the amenities section of each comp and count the total listed. Note the ones that appear in every top performer, then add the gaps to your listing today. This is the longest step, at about 8 minutes. Our guide to amenity completeness explains why every blank field costs you rank.

Pro tip: Complete amenity fields help you appear in filtered searches, and guests use those filters.

Common mistake: Listing generic amenities like wifi and parking without the speed, type or quantity, which makes you invisible in filtered searches.

How do I tell a pricing problem from a visibility problem?

Record each comp's price for your search dates and calculate the average. Note how many reviews each one has received in the last 90 days. If your price is 10% or more above the comp set average and your review velocity is slower, you have a conversion problem, not a visibility problem. This takes about 5 minutes.

Pro tip: Recent reviews matter as well as the total, because they show the listing is active.

Common mistake: Pricing 20% or more above your comp set without a Guest Favorite badge or Superhost status to justify the premium. It kills your conversion rate.

What will a comp set analysis tell me?

You will know your exact ranking position, who is outranking you, and the 3 to 5 specific gaps in photos, amenities or pricing behind your low conversion. Most hosts find 2 to 3 quick fixes they can make the same day.

Frequently asked questions

How many listings should be in an Airbnb comp set?

Five to seven that match your bedroom count, property type, location and price range.

How often should I check my comp set?

Weekly for your next 60 days. Comp sets change as listings book up.

Should I search logged in or logged out?

Logged out, or in a private window, so you see what guests see.

What should I compare first?

Cover photo and title, then price for the same dates, then amenities and recent reviews.

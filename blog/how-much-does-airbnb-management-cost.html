How Much Does Airbnb Management Cost?

Full-service managers, co-hosts, revenue managers and software: how each one charges, and what you get for it.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 27, 2026

Full-service Airbnb managers usually charge a percentage of booking revenue, and each sets its own rate. Co-hosts on Airbnb show their pricing on their profile and can be paid the cleaning fee, a percentage, or a fixed amount per booking. Revenue-only services and software cost less because they do less: PriceLabs starts at $19.99 per listing per month, and RevWhisper charges a flat monthly retainer per listing for pricing, rank tracking and listing work. The right choice depends on whether you need operations handled or only revenue.

What does a full-service Airbnb manager charge?

Most full-service managers charge a percentage of booking revenue, and each one sets its own rate, so compare written quotes. Ask which of these the fee covers: guest messaging, cleaning coordination, maintenance, pricing and listing management. Also ask whether onboarding, maintenance or restocking are billed on top of the percentage.

What does an Airbnb co-host charge?

It depends on the co-host. On Airbnb, co-hosts choose which services they offer, from guest messaging and on-site support to pricing, cleaning, photography and full listing setup, and they show their pricing on their profile. Hosts can pay a co-host through Airbnb in one of four ways: the cleaning fee, the cleaning fee plus a percentage, a percentage of each payout, or a fixed amount. Airbnb works out a co-host's percentage from the host's earnings after the host service fee. Sources: Airbnb's Find the right co-host and co-host payouts.

Is a percentage fee or a flat fee better?

A percentage fee grows with your revenue. A flat fee stays the same, so more of any increase stays with you. A percentage fee also earns the manager nothing when the property sits empty, which some owners prefer.

Pro tip: Ask what the percentage is calculated on: gross bookings, or bookings after cleaning fees and Airbnb's fee. For co-hosts paid through Airbnb, the percentage comes from your earnings after Airbnb's host service fee.

Which option should I choose?

If cleaning and guests are handled, revenue is usually the gap, so a revenue manager or software fits. If you need everything run, a full-service manager fits. See Remote Revenue Management vs a Local Airbnb Manager.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage do Airbnb managers take?

It varies, and Airbnb doesn't publish a standard rate. Full-service managers set their own percentage. Co-hosts on Airbnb show their pricing on their profile, and hosts can pay them the cleaning fee, a percentage, or a fixed amount per booking. Compare quotes on the same revenue base and the same services.

Are there hidden fees with Airbnb management companies?

Ask for a written fee schedule that lists anything billed outside the percentage, such as onboarding, maintenance or restocking.

Is Airbnb's 15.5% fee included in management fees?

No. Airbnb's host-only fee, 15.5% for most hosts, is charged separately. For co-hosts paid through Airbnb, their share is worked out after the host service fee is subtracted.

Why does RevWhisper charge a flat fee?

A flat monthly retainer per listing means the fee doesn't rise as revenue rises.

How do I know if management is worth it?

Compare the fee with the extra revenue it brings in, using the same months last year as the baseline.

2027 Holiday Calendar for Airbnb Hosts: Dates to Price Now

Every major US holiday in 2027, the day it falls on, and which ones create long weekends. Set these rates before guests start booking.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated October 29, 2026

2027 has six Monday federal holidays that create long weekends, plus Thanksgiving's four-day weekend on November 25 and New Year's Day on a Friday. Independence Day falls on Sunday, July 4, so the holiday is observed Monday, July 5. Set holiday rates and minimum stays for these dates now, before guests book.

Which 2027 holidays create long weekends?

MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day and Columbus / Indigenous Peoples Day fall on Mondays, and Independence Day is observed Monday, July 5. Thanksgiving creates a four-day weekend. New Year's Day 2027 is a Friday, and New Year's Day 2028 is observed Friday, December 31, 2027.

What minimum stay should I set for holiday weekends?

Match it to the long weekend. A three-night minimum on a Monday holiday weekend keeps one-night bookings from breaking it up.

Pro tip: Price summer and winter holidays differently. A July long weekend at a lake and Presidents Day at the same lake are different weekends.

Which local events should I add to my 2027 calendar?

School breaks for the cities your guests drive from, festivals, races, graduations and conferences near you. These often move demand more than a national holiday.

When should I set 2027 holiday prices?

Now. Then let your pricing tool or revenue manager adjust as bookings come in, and check your rates against your comp set for the same dates.

Frequently asked questions

When is Thanksgiving 2027?

Thursday, November 25, 2027.

What day is July 4, 2027?

Sunday. The federal holiday is observed Monday, July 5, which creates a long weekend.

How early should I price holidays on Airbnb?

As early as possible. Guests planning holiday trips book ahead, and late changes can't catch demand that already booked elsewhere.

Should holiday weekends have a minimum stay?

Yes. Match the minimum to the length of the long weekend so it isn't split by one-night bookings.

How to Set Up PriceLabs for Airbnb

A setup guide from a team that runs PriceLabs every day: what to set first and what to check weekly.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 18, 2026

To set up PriceLabs for Airbnb, turn off Airbnb Smart Pricing, connect your Airbnb account (or your PMS if you use one), set a base price (your average nightly rate across the year), set minimum and maximum prices, then add minimum stay, gap night and last-minute rules before syncing rates. Review the calendar for the next 90 days before you turn sync on. After that, check rates against your comps every week.

What should I check before connecting PriceLabs to Airbnb?

Five things, per PriceLabs' own setup guide. If you use a property management system or channel manager, connect PriceLabs to that instead of Airbnb, so rates flow from PriceLabs to the PMS to Airbnb and Vrbo. If you used one before and stopped, make sure it's disconnected from your Airbnb account. The listing must be live and bookable, because listings that are snoozed, have no availability or are newly created can't be imported. If you're a co-host, you need Full Access permissions. And plan to stop using Airbnb's pricing tips on any listing PriceLabs will sync.

How do I connect PriceLabs to Airbnb?

  1. In Airbnb, turn off Smart Pricing so it doesn't conflict with PriceLabs rates. If you've switched to Airbnb's new dynamic pricing, which started rolling out in October 2026, turn that off too so only PriceLabs sets your rates.
  2. If you have rulesets on Airbnb, delete them or skip check-in and checkout restrictions in PriceLabs, or the sync may fail.
  3. In PriceLabs, click Add your listings (or Add/Reconnect Listings), choose Airbnb/Houfy/Vrbo, then Airbnb.
  4. Click Connect with Airbnb and approve access on Airbnb's site.
  5. Your listings appear in the PriceLabs dashboard. Set them up before turning on sync.

PriceLabs can send daily rates, minimum stays and check-in and checkout restrictions to Airbnb up to 540 days ahead. Source: PriceLabs Help Center, How to Integrate PriceLabs with Airbnb.

Pro tip: Once sync is on, PriceLabs overwrites any prices set from Airbnb's pricing tips, so stop using pricing tips on synced listings.

How do I check that PriceLabs is syncing to Airbnb?

Turn on Sync Prices, or click Sync Now to push immediately. Then open Airbnb: your PriceLabs rates show on the Airbnb calendar, and minimum stays and check-in and checkout rules show under Settings, Availability, Custom Trip Lengths. Airbnb notes that changes made directly on Airbnb might override the rates or availability your software sends, and that a promotion or discount set directly on Airbnb won't show up in your software.

What base price should I set in PriceLabs?

Your average nightly rate across the whole year, not your peak price and not your floor. That's how PriceLabs defines base price, and it adds weekend and seasonal changes on top. PriceLabs' base price help tool can suggest one from a comp set of at least 30 listings. RevWhisper also checks it against the 5 to 7 listings that match your bedroom count, property type and location most closely. See How to Run a Comp Set Analysis in 30 Minutes.

What minimum and maximum prices should I set?

A floor you won't go below even on slow dates, and a ceiling high enough not to block peak weekends and holidays. Reset both by season. PriceLabs' Custom Seasonal Prices setting lets you set seasonal minimum, base and maximum prices.

Common mistake: Leaving the maximum too low. Peak dates hit the ceiling and sell for less than the market would pay.

How often should I check PriceLabs?

Weekly for the next 60 to 90 days, against your comp set. Daily during peak season if you can.

Pro tip: Before turning on sync, scroll the next 90 days in PriceLabs and look for rates that would surprise a guest.

Yes. PriceLabs says Smart Pricing must always be off when syncing, because it overrides the rates PriceLabs pushes. Only one tool should control your rates.

Airbnb Discounts for Hosts: Weekly, Monthly and the New 2026 Guest Discounts

Every discount Airbnb offers hosts in 2026, which one applies when several overlap, and when each one pays off.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 23, 2026

Airbnb lets hosts offer weekly and monthly discounts, early-bird and last-minute discounts, custom promotions, a 20% new listing promotion that runs until 3 guest reviews or 10 bookings, and, new in 2026, a 15% discount for top-rated guests. When the five main discounts overlap, only one applies, in a set order with the new listing promotion first; the top-rated guest discount can be added on top. Use them on dates that would otherwise sit empty, not across the whole calendar.

What discounts can Airbnb hosts offer?

DiscountHow it works
New listing promotion20% off until a new listing gets 3 guest reviews or 10 bookings, whichever comes first. Smart Pricing must be off
Weekly and monthlyPercent off stays of 7+ or 28+ nights
Early-birdPercent off bookings made 1 to 24 months before check-in
Last-minutePercent off bookings confirmed 1 to 28 days before check-in. Not available while Smart Pricing is on
Custom promotionPercent off dates you choose, once the listing has at least 3 bookings, for dates less than 90 days away
Top-rated guest15% for guests rated 4.8+ with at least 3 host reviews. Can be added on top of other discounts

After any discount, your nightly price must stay at least $10, and discounts apply to the nightly rate, not cleaning or service fees. Sources: Airbnb's How discounts are applied and Combining discounts, 2026.

Which Airbnb discount applies when several overlap?

Of the five main discounts, only one applies per reservation, in this order: new listing promotion, custom promotion, length-of-stay discount, early-bird, then last-minute. A custom promotion overrides a monthly discount for the same dates, per Airbnb's own example. Two things can still be added on top: the top-rated guest discount, and Airbnb's 10% non-refundable discount, which applies after all other discounts. Airbnb's own monthly stay savings, which Airbnb pays for, can also combine with a discount.

Should I offer a monthly discount on Airbnb?

In slow months, often yes. Long stays are a real share of demand: Airbnb says nearly one in five nights booked is part of a stay of 28 nights or more. Make the monthly discount deeper than the weekly one, so a month costs less per night than a week.

Should I turn on the top-rated guest discount?

Test it on slow dates. Airbnb estimates hosts who offered similar discounts to top-rated guests earned 19% more and had 24% more nights booked on average, based on an Airbnb test from July 31 to August 30, 2026. That's directional, not a guarantee, and because it can be added on top of your other discounts, check what a stacked discount does to your nightly rate. See How to Price Your Airbnb.

Do discounts help my Airbnb ranking?

Discounts of 10% or more show guests a strikethrough price in search results and on your listing, and Airbnb says adding a discount may also help your ranking. They don't fix a listing priced above its comps or a weak cover photo.

Pro tip: Price the date right first, then use a discount to fill a specific gap.

Frequently asked questions

What is Airbnb's new listing promotion?

A 20% discount that runs until a new listing gets 3 guest reviews or 10 bookings, whichever comes first. The listing needs Smart Pricing turned off.

Can Airbnb discounts stack?

Partly. Only one of the five main discounts applies per reservation, in a set order. The top-rated guest discount can be added on top.

What is the top-rated guest discount?

A 15% discount hosts can offer to guests rated 4.8 or higher with at least 3 host reviews.

Should monthly discounts be bigger than weekly?

Usually, yes. A deeper monthly discount means a month costs less per night than a week, which is what monthly guests expect.

Do discounts help Airbnb ranking?

Airbnb says adding a discount may help your ranking, and discounts of 10% or more show guests a strikethrough price in search results.

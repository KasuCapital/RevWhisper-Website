Pricing Airbnb Stays of 28 Nights or More

Monthly stays fill slow months but run on their own discount, cancellation, payout and tax rules. When to accept them and how to price them.

By Marisa @ RevWhisperLast updated November 26, 2026

Airbnb treats stays of 28 nights or more as long-term: they use your monthly discount and your separate long-term cancellation policy (Firm or Strict). Most places still tax a 28-night stay; some lodging taxes stop after a set length, but the cutoff varies by state and city. Price them below your nightly rate but above what an empty month earns, and set the monthly discount at least as deep as the weekly one. They suit slow months, not peak season.

How does Airbnb handle stays of 28 nights or more?

They use your monthly length-of-stay discount and your long-term cancellation policy, which is separate from your short-stay policy. They're a real share of demand: Airbnb says stays of 28 nights or more made up 21% of nights booked in 2022.

How should I price a monthly Airbnb stay?

StepWhat to do
Start from the month's expected revenueWhat the month earns at your normal occupancy
Subtract costs you saveFewer cleanings and turnovers
Set the monthly discountAt least as deep as the weekly discount
Check against an empty monthAccept if it beats what the month would earn otherwise

When should I accept monthly stays?

In slow months and shoulder seasons, when a full month at a discount beats a patchy calendar. Avoid them across peak weeks, holidays and events, where nightly stays earn more.

Do monthly Airbnb stays pay lodging taxes?

Usually, yes. Some lodging taxes stop applying once a stay passes a set length, but the cutoff varies by state and city, and a 28-night stay is still taxed in most places. A few examples from Airbnb's own tax pages:

WhereWhat Airbnb collects on longer stays
TexasState hotel tax on stays of 29 nights or fewer
ArizonaState transaction privilege tax on stays of 29 nights or fewer
New YorkSales tax on New York City stays of up to 179 nights, and up to 89 nights elsewhere in the state
Florida6% transient rental tax on stays of up to 182 nights

Check Airbnb's tax page for your state, and your city and county rules. In New York City, rentals of 30 consecutive days or more are exempt from the short-term rental registration requirement, but Airbnb still collects New York sales tax on those stays. See New York Airbnb Law Explained.

How do payouts work for monthly Airbnb stays?

For reservations of 28 nights or more, Airbnb's Payments Terms say the first payout goes out by the end of the business day after the guest's check-in date, and later payouts follow every 30 days for the rest of the stay. New hosts are an exception: if you haven't completed two stays or verified your listing, the first and second payouts generally go out together, 28 days after check-in. Guests on long stays may pay in recurring installments rather than all at once.

What cancellation policy applies to monthly stays?

Your long-term policy, which you set separately from your short-stay policy. Your options are Firm and Strict. Under long-term Firm, guests get a full refund up to 30 days before check-in; after that, you're paid for nights spent plus 30 more nights. See Airbnb Cancellation Policies for Hosts.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a long-term stay on Airbnb?

Stays of 28 nights or more.

What discount should I give for a monthly Airbnb stay?

Enough to win the booking while beating what the month would earn otherwise, and at least as deep as your weekly discount.

Do Airbnb monthly guests pay taxes?

Usually, yes. Some taxes stop after a set stay length, but the cutoff varies: Airbnb collects Texas state hotel tax only on stays of 29 nights or fewer, but collects Florida's 6% transient rental tax on stays of up to 182 nights. Check Airbnb's tax page for your state.

Which cancellation policy applies to monthly stays?

Your separate long-term cancellation policy.

Should I accept monthly stays in peak season?

Usually not. Nightly stays earn more on peak dates.

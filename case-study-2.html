The math, from the bookings. $38.3K is already on the forward books, with $28.0K of inventory still open. The first month shows the listing capturing the calendar fast, so we model about 60 percent of that open inventory filling through the year, weighted toward the higher-rate winter that is still wide open. That lands the forward year near $55K gross. Subtract the fee of $500 a month, $6K, and the owner nets about $49K. That clears last year by roughly $9.3K, about 23 percent on the net and close to 40 percent on the gross book. If winter fills the way summer did, the number runs higher, toward $60K.

We bring the demand. The host keeps it five stars.

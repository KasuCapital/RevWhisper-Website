Priced by a professional. Invisible in search.

The fifth lever

We set you up for success. You determine the outcome.

Mary did not come to us cold. When we met at a conference, she already had a professional revenue manager working her portfolio. The homes were excellent, the guests loved them, and someone competent was setting the price. On paper there was no reason to change a thing.

She changed something anyway. She let us take two of her best homes and run them on our system, at a rate we set below standard so the comparison would be honest. That was the leap. Not faith in a promise, faith in a measurement, taken against another professional's numbers.

We did our part. Backend, content, gallery, and price as a position in search. The listings moved from page four to page one on the dates that carry the rate, the forward book is up 72 percent on what a professional had on the books a year ago, and twenty reservations came in over roughly six weeks. The leap is paying off, which is why the rest of the portfolio is on its way across.

Here is the part no software can do for you. We set you up for success. You determine the outcome. Every booking we win lands a guest in Mary's home, and Mary keeps sending them away five stars. That is the fifth lever, the one we can never pull for her. Visibility becomes reviews, reviews become rank, rank becomes the next booking won for less. Keep delivering the stay these reviews describe, and this does not simply continue. It compounds.
